# Erin Ton Smashes Women's Aconcagua Record by Nearly Two Hours

**American ultrarunner sets blistering 6:33 roundtrip time on South America's highest peak**

Erin Ton has added another remarkable achievement to her already staggering FKT resume, setting the women's fastest known time on Aconcagua with a roundtrip time of 6 hours 33 minutes on January 28th.

The 22,841-foot peak in Argentina - the highest in South America and the Western Hemisphere - saw Ton demolish the previous women's record by nearly two hours. Slovakia's Lenka Poláčková had set the supported women's mark of 8:17 just weeks earlier in January.

Ton completed the roughly 8.7-mile roundtrip with 8,500 feet of elevation gain entirely unsupported, reaching the summit in just 4 hours 50 minutes before a rapid descent. She reportedly spent around 10 minutes on the summit before heading down.

"There were murmurs around camp that the actual time had been set by a guide at 7:35 round trip, so this was the time to beat," Ton said of her target going into the attempt.

The Aconcagua FKTs mark numbers 180 and 181 in Ton's verified collection - a staggering tally that includes her 2024 Colorado 14ers record, where she summited 57 of the state's 58 peaks over 14,000 feet in just 14 days, 10 hours and 9 minutes.

Ton isn't done with Aconcagua either. She's reportedly eyeing a longer 40-mile variant with 13,250 feet of elevation gain, and a cryptic "1/7 summits done" comment suggests the Seven Summits may be in her sights.
